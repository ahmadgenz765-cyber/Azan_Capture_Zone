How to Choose Perfect Lighting for Outdoor Weddings

Lighting is the single most defining factor in photography. When planning an outdoor wedding, understanding how sunlight changes throughout the day can make the difference between harsh shadows and warm, romantic portraits.

1. The Magic of Golden Hour

Golden hour occurs during the last hour before sunset. During this time, the sun is low on the horizon, casting soft, warm light that flatters skin tones and adds a golden glow to your wedding portraits.

2. Avoiding Harsh Midday Sun

Direct sunlight between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM creates deep shadows under the eyes and nose. If your ceremony takes place during these hours, look for shaded outdoor areas or utilize diffused backdrop tents.

3. Incorporating Ambient & String Lights

As dusk settles into night, fairy lights, lanterns, and warm floodlights create a magical night atmosphere for your reception film clips and candid dance floor shots.
